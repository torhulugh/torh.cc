Art gunt app

Art Hunt Project is an innovative mobile app meticulously crafted to ignite a newfound passion for art. Immerse yourself in an augmented reality-infused journey that transforms static artworks into dynamic, interactive masterpieces. Picture paintings that come alive, responding to your touch and creating an experience that seamlessly blends with the trends of our technology-driven age. Set against the vibrant backdrop of Toronto, the Art Hunt app serves as a digital gateway to a treasure trove of undiscovered artistic gems scattered throughout the city. Art Hunt app is more than just a virtual gallery—it's a gamified adventure waiting to unfold. Engage in a thrilling experience with a reward system that beckons you to explore local stores across Toronto.

User Goals

  • Using AR Technology in a more exciting medium.
  • Finding fun new ways to explore art galleries and discovering new artiest and places in the city.

Business Objectives

  • My task was to design a promotion for Food menu for social media promotion

Problem Statement

  • How might we increase engagement, and foot traffic, at Yonge Dundas square and also encouraging patronage of local business around the square.

Solution

  • Mobile application
  • Augmented Reality technology
  • Gamification and reward system
